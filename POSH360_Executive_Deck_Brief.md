## Design Guidelines for All Slides

1. **Typography:**
   - Headlines: Bold, Deep Blue (#1B3A6B), 48-56pt
   - Sub-headlines: Dark Gray, 24-28pt
   - Body: Dark Gray (#2C3E50), 16-18pt
   - Use max 2 font families (e.g., Montserrat + Open Sans)

2. **Spacing:**
   - Generous white space between sections
   - Minimal cluttering
   - Consistent margins (0.75 inches)

3. **Icons & Graphics:**
   - Use professional, flat-design icons
   - Accent colors for emphasis (red for warnings, gold for achievements)
   - Avoid complex visuals; keep it minimal

4. **Color Usage:**
   - Deep Blue: Headlines, key messages
   - White: Backgrounds, cards
   - Red/Orange: Warnings, urgency (Slide 2, CTA)
   - Gold: Achievements, statistics (Slide 5)
   - Gray: Supporting text

5. **Consistency:**
   - Same header style on all slides
   - Consistent footer with logo/contact info
   - Uniform card designs for testimonials and key points
   - Professional, not flashy

6. **Tone:**
   - Professional and authoritative
   - Urgent but not alarmist (fear is a motivator, not a manipulator)
   - Solutions-focused (every problem has a POSH360 answer)
   - CEO/Senior Management language (avoid jargon, focus on business impact)
