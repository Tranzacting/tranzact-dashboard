## 📌 TL;DR — Read this even if you read nothing else

**TranZact turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory — run by AI, managed by people, regardless of size.** This is our canonical opening line. Lead with it everywhere — hero, homepage, top of any copy.

In plain words: **TranZact is the Factory AI OS that turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory.** AI runs your Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Production; your team manage the AI. We believe in the **AI-Run Factory** — a new kind of factory where AI runs operations and people manage the AI, regardless of size.

This is our **movement**, and it’s centerstage in everything we say. *Why a movement, not a product?* Because the companies that win big in any industry don’t just sell products — they build movements customers join. Apple didn’t just sell computers; it built *“Think Different”* — a movement for creative rebels. Tesla didn’t just sell cars; it built a movement to *accelerate the world’s transition to sustainable energy*. Customers buy products, but they *join* movements. The **AI-Run Factory** is ours.

The **Factory AI OS** is the software category that powers the movement — but we use it minimally. Lead with the movement; name the category only when copy must explain *what the product specifically is* (the homepage descriptor, investors, press, analysts). In the same way that **Salesforce defined the CRM category** and **SAP defined the ERP category**, TranZact is defining the **Factory AI OS** category. But we don’t lead with the category. We lead with the movement.

Our approach is the **Simon Sinek Golden Circle**: **Lead with WHY. Explain with HOW. Mention WHAT last.**

- 💡 **WHY** (the movement) — *We believe in the AI-Run Factory.*
- ⚡ **HOW** (the mechanism) — *Run by AI, managed by people, regardless of size.*
- 📦 **WHAT** (the category, minimum use) — *The Factory AI OS.*

Our positioning headline is ***“Run by AI, managed by people, regardless of size.”*** Our sticky line — what we want every customer to repeat — is ***“I’m turning my factory into an AI-Run Factory — run by AI, managed by my people.”***

**Zero agent names in written copy** — agents live only in sales decks. *(One ratified exception: “AI Teammates,” which reads as a category, not a named agent.)* 🌍 **Born in India. Building for the world.**

## 🆕 v3.5 — The Locked Rules (read these before writing)

1. **Lead written copy with the canonical opening line:** *“TranZact turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory — run by AI, managed by people, regardless of size.”*
2. **Product descriptor:** *“TranZact is the Factory AI OS that turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory.”* This states what the product *is* — sales intros, elevator, cold email, expo, product one-liner. In cold first-touch, lead with *“turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory”* and support it with the functional line — *“AI runs your Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Production and your team manage the AI”* (or the enquiry-to-dispatch variant). *(“AI that runs your factory” is retired.)*
3. **Factory AI OS — minimum use.** Lead with the movement. Name the category only when copy must explain what the product specifically is (homepage descriptor) or for investor/press/analyst contexts.
4. **Zero agent names in written copy.** Sole exception: **“AI Teammates.”**
5. **The four operations we name:** **Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Production.** Not quality, finance, or planning.
6. **Functional line — required in any descriptor copy over one line.** Include one of:
    - *“AI runs your Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Production and your team manage the AI.”*
    - *“AI runs your entire operations from enquiry to dispatch and your team manage the AI.”*
7. **Default proof point is “10,000+ manufacturers.”** Customer names (SH Engitech, Mesco Spring) live in testimonials, case studies, and proof-specific/long-form copy — **never in 1–2 liners, hero, or ads.**
8. **“Context graph” is investor/talent language only.** For customers, use *“Built on 7 years of learning from 10,000+ manufacturers.”*
9. **Don’t overload “factory.”** Manufacturer/manufacturing outweighs factory in body copy. Locked terms (AI-Run Factory) are exempt.
10. **Funding:** $8M **total** = $7M Series A + $1M seed. Default copy: *“$8M in funding from [investors].”* **Never write “$8M Series A.”**
11. **Tally co-existence:** sales and objection-handling, **plus relatable brand-context use** (ratified). Never in investor/press copy.

# 🎨 APPENDIX A — The Voice Guide

**TranZact sounds like:** Bold prophet, plain words. Apple-meets-Tesla energy, grounded in the reality of Indian manufacturing.

### ✅ Do

- ✂️ Short sentences. Real words.
- 🎯 Generational claims. Simple language.
- 🏭 Name the cage, then break it.
- 🪧 Lead written copy with: *“TranZact turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory — run by AI, managed by people, regardless of size.”*
- ⚙️ In any descriptor copy over one line, include the functional line: *“AI runs your Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Production and your team manage the AI”* — or — *“AI runs your entire operations from enquiry to dispatch and your team manage the AI.”*
- 💪 Let the product do the heavy lifting with specifics. Default proof: **10,000+ manufacturers.** Named customers (SH Engitech, Mesco) for testimonials and proof-specific copy only.
- 🌍 *“Born in India. Building for the world.”* — use as a closing/signature line in customer, investor, press, and talent copy.
- 💡 Lead with WHY (AI-Run Factory). Explain with HOW. Mention WHAT (Factory AI OS) last, and only when it explains what the product specifically is.

### 🚫 Don’t

- 🚫 Jargon (*“synergy,” “leverage,” “enablement,” “solutioning”*).
- 🚫 Hedge words (*“potentially,” “could help,” “we aim to”*).
- 🚫 Feature lists without outcomes.
- 🚫 Sound like another B2B SaaS.
- 🚫 Lead with technology. Always lead with the owner, the cage, and the outcome.
- 🚫 Name any agents (Sales Agent, Purchase Agent, Data Agent, etc.) in written marketing copy. *Sole exception: “AI Teammates.”*
- 🚫 Say **“context graph”** to customers — it’s investor/talent language. For customers: *“Built on 7 years of learning from 10,000+ manufacturers.”*
- 🚫 Overload **“factory.”** Manufacturer/manufacturing outweighs factory in body copy. Locked terms (AI-Run Factory) are exempt.
- 🚫 Put **customer names** in 1–2 liners, hero, or ads. Use 10,000+ manufacturers.
- 🚫 Write **“$8M Series A.”** $8M is total ($7M Series A + $1M seed).
- 🚫 Collapse movement into category. TranZact is the AI that turns your factory into an AI-Run Factory. Customers become the AI-Run Factory. Never flip these.

### ✅ Voice Checklist (before publishing any copy)

- [ ]  Would SH Engitech’s owner nod reading this?
- [ ]  Is every sentence under 25 words?
- [ ]  Does it sound like a prophet, not a salesman?
- [ ]  Could a 30-year-old next-gen owner AND a 45-year-old engineer-owner both understand it?
- [ ]  Does it lead with WHY (the AI-Run Factory) before mentioning HOW or WHAT?
- [ ]  If it’s over one line, is the functional line in it?
- [ ]  Is the proof “10,000+ manufacturers” (names reserved for testimonials/proof-specific copy)?
- [ ]  Is “context graph” kept out of customer copy?
- [ ]  Does manufacturer/manufacturing outweigh factory?
- [ ]  Has any agent been named (other than “AI Teammates”)? If yes — remove.
